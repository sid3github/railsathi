# Video script (read this out loud)

Simple words, short sentences. Read at a normal, calm pace — you have buffer built in.
**[Bracketed lines]** are actions (what to click). Everything else is what you say.

Total spoken words: ~220. At a normal reading speed that is about 1:40 — well under
the 2:00 limit, so don't rush.

---

### MINUTE 1 — show the app

**[Open the homepage. Type the PNR.]**
"Hi, I'm Sid. This is RailSathi."
"It helps train passengers when their train is cancelled."
"I type my PNR number. No login. No password."

**[Click "View my plan."]**
"One tap, and I see my problem."

**[Cancellation screen shows.]**
"My train is cancelled. RailSathi explains this in simple words, and says my money is safe."

**[Scroll to the three option cards.]**
"Now I get three choices: take another train, get a refund, or talk to a person."

**[Click "See train options." Pick a different train.]**
"I choose another train. I see the time, the seat, and the extra cost — before I pay anything."

**[Click "Confirm my new plan."]**
"One tap, and my new ticket is confirmed. My refund is already on its way."

**[Tap the language button (हि). Let it switch on screen.]**
"The whole app also works in Hindi. Not just a few words. Everything."

---

### MINUTE 2 — how it was built

**[Show the code editor or the Codex session.]**
"I built this using Codex. It helped me write the code and design every screen."

**[Show the network panel, or just say the numbers on screen.]**
"I also made it fast. It started at one point two megabytes. Now it loads in under three hundred kilobytes."
"This matters, because many Indian users have slow internet and simple phones."

**[Tab through a screen with the keyboard, so the focus outline shows.]**
"I checked accessibility too. You can use the whole app with just a keyboard."

**[Show the options screen again, or a simple sketch.]**
"This is more than a screen. In real life, this would connect to railway systems."
"When a train is cancelled, we send a link by SMS or WhatsApp. So people don't have to search — the message comes to them."

**[Show the "synthetic data" note on screen.]**
"All the data in this demo is fake. It is just for this test."
"This is an independent project. It is not made by Indian Railways."

"Thank you."

## Practice tips

- Read it out loud once before recording, without the camera. Time yourself.
- If you stumble on a line, just re-record that section — you don't need one perfect take.
- Say numbers as words, the way you'd say them out loud: "one point two megabytes," not "1.2 MB."
- Pause half a second after clicking something, before you speak the next line — it looks calmer, and it's easier for you to stay in sync with the screen.
