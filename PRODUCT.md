# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Indian rail passengers whose booked journey has been delayed, cancelled, or disrupted, including people using a phone under time pressure and with limited confidence in complex digital services.

## Product Purpose

RailSathi turns an unclear journey disruption into a clear next-step plan. Success means a passenger can understand what changed, choose a safe alternative, and leave with a confirmed plan or visible refund status.

## Positioning

Rather than making a passenger decode railway status messages, RailSathi translates the disruption into a calm, personalised decision path with the trade-offs stated plainly.

## Operating Context

The hackathon prototype demonstrates one synthetic Mumbai-to-Delhi cancellation journey. The citizen flow—not an admin workflow—is the primary product surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The main journey supports disruption explanation, alternate-train selection, a protected-fare decision, refund tracking, sharing a plan, and a support-callback simulation. All PNRs, availability, seats, refunds, and support outcomes are synthetic. It must not request or use real passwords, OTPs, payment data, Aadhaar/PAN data, or live government-system access. The project is independent and must not imply affiliation with Indian Railways or IRCTC.

## Brand Commitments

The product is called RailSathi. Its voice is calm, direct, reassuring, and respectful of a passenger's stress. The user requested a modern, clean, simple, premium production-grade experience and a train visual recognisably grounded in contemporary Indian rail travel.

## Evidence on Hand

The working prototype contains a labelled synthetic demo journey. The project has no verified live railway integration, testimonials, adoption claims, or official approval.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action unmistakable.
- Explain the consequence of every choice before asking for commitment.
- Treat safety and transparency as part of the experience.
- Design for a hurried mobile passenger first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear language, accessible contrast, responsive layouts, keyboard-operable controls, and an English/Hindi language affordance.
